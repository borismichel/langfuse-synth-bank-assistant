Judge comparison

Compare one overall judge with five policy judges on the same 22 frozen answers, approved evidence and conduct criteria. Both use the selected model on the deployment’s primary provider. Reference labels are constructed fixtures, not independent human reviews.

The matched comparison makes at most 132 model calls. Including the separate legacy rubric makes at most 154 calls. The legacy rubric has different instructions and omits approved context; it is excluded from matched agreement.

One request runs at a time, with a 10-minute deadline, a 30-second request timeout, no provider retries and at most 500 output tokens per call. Depot checks current spending before each dispatch. Its hourly estimate is not a billing reservation. Remote managed evaluations are separate.

Cancellation stops new calls. An in-flight call can finish and remain billable. Repeating an action uses its existing result; interrupted or uncertain actions require native inspection before a new action. Runtime receipts last for this container’s lifetime; native results remain in Langfuse.
