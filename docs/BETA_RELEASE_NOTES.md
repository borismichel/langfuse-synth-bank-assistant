## Guided use

Follow the [presenter runbook](../DEMO_SCRIPT.md). Begin with imported history and a single bilingual banking conversation. Confirm synthetic banking actions explicitly. Optional voice, complaint and comparison controls incur provider costs; plan one bounded exercise at a time, preserve observed failures, and do not retry to obtain a passing result.

Keep the same instance for comparison and release receipts. Save evidence and pause owned managed judges before stopping it. Never promote without an eligible passed comparison for the exact distinct candidate and current production version.

Catalogue screenshots are labelled model-free fixtures. They do not prove current-image provider execution, audio quality, human acceptance or governance permissions. Beta publication does not close unmet qualification requirements.
